The IAPETUS was founded in 2008 by Tye Hilfiger and Marq Pellock after seeing the horrific treatment of prisoners. Their goal was to make medical care so accessible that nobody would have to suffer as they and many inmates did under harsh conditions. We started as a small pharmaceutical store, and have expanded into research that intends to save the human condition! Under the new head executive, Alise Sepius the company has strove to new prospects never before dreamed of! If you would like to learn more about our staff, click here.
